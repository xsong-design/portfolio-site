## Meta pages (unlocked)

Both are long PNG case studies (Ads Manager UI), Ads Team + Business Messaging. Shared program: integrating Marketing Messages (0-to-1) into Ads Manager. **Xiao states they left the company and have no MVP results to share.**

### Integrated Campaign Creation (`/meta-budget-comprehension`)

- Role: Product Designer, Business Messaging sprint, **budgeting track**
- Timeline: **Q4 2022 – Q3 2024**
- Scope: ad creation flow — how new Marketing Messages features change advertiser experience
- Stated goals: incremental WhatsApp revenue **$1.4B by 2026**; increase ad + messaging spend; help advertisers hit business goals
- Problem: MM beta was a **separate tab**; advertisers had to create ads vs MM separately and **manually allocate budget**
- Research: bi-weekly sprints; two consecutive 10-week sprints; advertisers think of MM as **different from marketing-creation campaign**; worry they **cannot turn MM off**; **budgeting is another key concern**; optimizations may **not reach 100% delivery**
- Solution (screens exist): **persistent right-hand-rail card** on ad set (“your ad set includes marketing messages”); expandable how-MM-affects-controls; **inline guidance at budget level**; rail dismissible with two reappearances; inline card stays for budget comprehension
- Reflection: collaboration with Ads / Guidance / Vertical teams; better documentation next time

### Campaign Reporting (`/meta-reporting-experience`)

- Role: Product Designer, Business Messaging sprint, **reporting integration**
- Timeline: **Q4 2022 – Q3 2024**
- Scope: **post-publish** Ads Manager table — ads + marketing messages in **one table**
- Problem: temporary **isolated MM metrics tab**; switch between tables to judge performance
- Guiding principles: minimize disruption; consistency of metric definitions; minimize friction of aggregated vs distinct metrics; predictable actions
- Solution (screens exist): onboarding when advertiser **creates MM + ads as integrated campaign**; **Messages Delivered** metric; **opt in/out**; custom metrics; breakdowns (aggregated ads vs MM vs both); Search/Star/Group preserved
- Reflection: ads reporting teams were welcoming; more live working sessions vs decks; enhancement is a **stepping stone** to breakdowns
