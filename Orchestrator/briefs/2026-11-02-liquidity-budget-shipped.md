## Verbatim
> So, when talking about what we shipped, first they need to understand what L1, L2, and L3 are. L1 is the ad level, L2 is the set level, and L3 is the campaign level. So, what we shipped is on the L2 because what we changed when marketing messages integrated into ads, where the budget is actually affected is on the L2 level, where within the set you could have both ads and marketing messages. The reason why we shipped into L2 is because that's where the changes are. That's where the budget will actually be impacted. And we decided to ship with both an inline message at the budget section as well as a global kind of notification in the right hand rail. So, the right hand rail is a sticky rail. So, we wanted to keep that in there to let users know that this campaign now contains marketing messages and not just ads so that they know that. Budget is one of the few things that will change, will be impacted. And yeah, the right hand rail was mainly because we heard that some of the people, the advertisers that are creating the campaigns, are not the same people that potentially made the ads. So, step back one second. In order to have marketing messages within campaigns, Ads campaigns, you need to enable marketing messages through onboarding. And the people that are enabling this sometimes are not the same people that create the ads. So we wanted to have an information card in the right hand rail on the L2 to let the advertisers know that a setting has been toggled within your ad account that now includes marketing messages and tell them about everything that has changed and what to expect, which includes budget. And then the inline message is mainly when the advertiser gets the budget card. If they did not interact with the right hand rail or read the right hand rail, they'll still have a stopgap there where they will have a message that lets them know that budget will now include marketing messages and not only be for advertisers' ads.

## Intent
Define L1/L2/L3, ship on L2, rail + inline. Onboarding enablement vs creator. Professional voice. No extra old-site details Xiao did not say (two reappearances, etc.).
