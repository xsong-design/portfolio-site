# MML recommended structure — one program, two blocks

Xiao: Liquidity encompasses budget comprehension (campaign creation) **and** reporting. Both are major blocks of the larger effort. Recruiters should get the umbrella *and* the two problems without a wall of text.

## Recruiter path (~90 seconds)

If they only read: **title + subtitle + Overview + the two block titles + Impact bullets**.

Word budget for the whole page: **~900–1,100** (current live MDX is longer and still reads as one undifferentiated story).

## Heading map (keep current greyscale template)

| Section | Job | Length |
|---------|-----|--------|
| Hero (already in the page shell) | Name the program. Subtitle names both blocks. | Existing metadata |
| **1. The program** | What Liquidity is (ads + MM, one budget, Ads Manager). Three orgs. Your role: two of the major blocks, not the entire e2e. One line on what you did *not* own. | 120–150 words |
| **2. The shared problem** | One insight that makes both blocks necessary: advertisers think email/guaranteed send; the product optimizes like ads. Cite 9/10. One quote. | 120–150 words |
| **3. Block A — Budget at creation** | Problem → decision → what shipped. Transparency without control. Messages Delivered *estimate*. | 180–220 words + 1 UI |
| **4. Block B — Reporting after send** | Same model, later in the journey. Campaigns table, line item, Delivered Messages metric, why not auto-populate. | 180–220 words + 1 UI |
| **5. Why the two blocks are one story** | 3–4 sentences: creation sets expectation; reporting proves it. Without B, A is a promise. | 60–80 words |
| **6. Impact** | Alpha / Bombay / XYXX / OZiva. Program outcome, not “budget metric” vs “reporting metric” unless you have that split. | 80–100 words |
| **7. Reflection** | One beat (GTM earlier, or org alignment). | 60–80 words |

Drop from the live page: duplicate overview, long sprint process, 16-options, systems-thinking essay. Put those in a “if you want depth” line at most — not a third process chapter.

## Flow (one sentence per beat)

Liquidity is Meta’s bet to run ads and WhatsApp marketing messages as one optimized campaign. Advertisers didn’t trust that model. I owned two of the major blocks: **make the split understandable before publish** (budget / creation) and **make the same split visible after send** (reporting). Same mental-model gap, two moments in Ads Manager.

## What not to do

- Two case study URLs (Xiao: reporting is part of Liquidity).
- One long chronological diary (sprint → then budget → then reporting as an afterthought).
- Equal airtime for onboarding as a *third* block unless you want three; treat onboarding as setup inside Block A or one paragraph in The program.
- Restyle to the old Webflow look. Two `h2` blocks in the existing type system is enough: `01 Budget comprehension` / `02 Campaign reporting`.
