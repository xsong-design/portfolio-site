### Narrative Enrichments

- **Preselected tip design:** The presentation explicitly states the design defaults to the middle tip amount, calculated in the backend via cost of delivery from store basket. This was a deliberate decision to "best reward our drivers" and aligns with industry standard. Includes a custom option where customers can interact or change the amount.

- **Placement rationale (more detail):** "Checkout is complex — mixed basket with store and online shipping from warehouse. Tip only applies to delivery goods." The module was placed as the final step in checkout before the persistent order summary, as the only optional module. They explicitly chose NOT to sandwich it between mandatory steps.

- **"Store delivery tip" naming:** Worked with content partners to propose this title for clarity — ensuring customers understood the tip applied only to store delivery items.

- **Tipping as $0 interaction:** "In order to set to 0, customer must take an additional action" — deliberate friction for opting out.

- **Post-fulfillment tipping (24-hour window):** For customers who didn't tip pre-transaction, there was a post-fulfillment prompt to reconsider. However, it was deliberately nested on the order details page (within account → purchase history) requiring more effort to find — a reaction to "Instacart scandals" around tip-baiting where customers would set high tips then immediately reduce them.

- **GMV trade-off narrative:** "While many positives came out of the ramp, we saw -12 bps to GMV, a $22 million dollar impact. Collaborating with business leaders, we decided to launch anyways. Ultimately, 31% of driver earnings are derived through tipping — this means a major win for them." This is powerful trade-off storytelling.

- **HMW statement:** "How might we introduce driver tipping earlier in the shopping funnel, to best advocate for our drivers, whilst educating customers to feel comfortable and more willing to reward good service."

- **Three-sided problem framing:** Consumer (perishable goods, time-sensitive), Drivers (juggle multiple apps, choose based on convenience/benefits), Business (rapid growth, supply unable to meet demand)

- **Growth context:** "Back in 2020, Covid changed the way we lived. Health regulations tightened and people became more housebound causing grocery deliveries becoming more popular. Walmart set out to grow its delivery fleet to accommodate the growing demand."

- **Takeaway quote:** "Tipping is unique, the complexity of balance and collaboration. This project has taught me a lot about strategy and push back."

### Recommended Action
The current draft significantly underplays the impact. With $7M savings, a 45pp tip rate jump, and 7.5M more deliveries, this is a much stronger story. Rewrite the Impact section with real numbers and add the GMV trade-off as a nuanced "What I'd Do Differently" or "Trade-off" element.

## Meta Business Messaging / MML (`marketing-message-liquidity.md`)

The presentation content largely overlaps with the existing draft but provides a few additional angles:

### Additional Details

- **Role specificity:** "I led the budget track of work within the Business Messaging sprint team tasked with integrating Marketing Messages into Ads Manager"
- **Revenue figure:** "$1.4 billion incremental revenue for WhatsApp alone" — the current draft mentions "$3.5B by 2030" as the opportunity. This $1.4B figure may refer to a different metric; verify with Xiao.
- **HMW statement:** "How might we orientate our advertisers to the changes made in the creation flow, and educate them on how Meta's optimization will allocate their budget across the various placements of ads and marketing messages"
- **Guidance design detail:** "The first guidance introduced is a persistent card on the right-hand rail, ensuring advertisers don't overlook changes from enabling Marketing Messages. Visible upon entering the ad set page, it informs of control updates and provides explanation on how to disable MM if it does not align with their strategy."
- **Inline guidance:** "The right-hand guidance is complemented by an inline card at the budget level. Since optimizations handle allocation, no control changes are needed. This specifically addresses concerns about how budget will be distributed."
- **Reflection:** "Collaboration was a key success factor, with designers, cross-functional partners and stakeholders and teams working seamlessly. The Ads, Guidance, and Vertical teams provided valuable input and ideas throughout. Next time, I would improve documentation of decisions. With leadership from different orgs reviewing in a fast-moving sprint, where iterations are constant, tracking changes was challenging."
- **Final thought:** "With the MVP launch, I hope the team continues to prioritize budget comprehension. It should extend beyond the creation flow and be integrated into the entire experience. Budget understanding is crucial for advertiser success."
- **Sprint process:** "Partnered closely with guidance designers" and "Campaign Creation - Set creation" were specific collaboration points

### Recommended Action
The existing MML draft is already strong and well-structured. These details are minor additions — the reflection about documentation and the HMW statement could be woven in if Xiao wants more depth. The $1.4B figure needs verification against the $3.5B already cited.
