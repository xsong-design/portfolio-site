## When nothing has released

- **The surface.** Meta Business Suite, not “the platform.”
- **Who it is for.** A small or medium business.
- **The judgment.** What the flow does in the moment, a holiday or otherwise.
- **The constraint.** Without Ads Manager. Through the store’s API. The verb stays “would,” because it had not released.

Keep “unreleased” in this file. It does not go on the resume. The public line simply does not claim a launch.
