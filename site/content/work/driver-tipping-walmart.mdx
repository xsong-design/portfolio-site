## Overview

Driver Tipping at Walmart reimagined how customers tip delivery drivers during the online grocery checkout and post-delivery experience. The project balanced the needs of three stakeholders — customers seeking transparency, drivers depending on fair compensation, and the business aiming for a sustainable delivery model.

## Context & Problem

Walmart's delivery tipping experience was underperforming. Customers were confused about when and how tips were distributed, drivers felt the system was opaque, and tip rates lagged behind competitors. The existing flow buried tipping in the checkout process with poor defaults and no post-delivery option.

Key challenges included:
- Low tip attachment rate compared to industry benchmarks
- Customer confusion about tip timing and distribution
- No ability to adjust tips after delivery based on experience
- Lack of transparency eroding trust with both customers and drivers

## Process

This project required balancing quantitative business metrics with qualitative human factors across a compressed timeline.

### Research & Discovery
- Analyzed tipping data across 6 months of delivery orders
- Conducted 10 customer interviews focused on tipping mental models
- Reviewed driver feedback surveys and NPS verbatims
- Competitive audit of DoorDash, Instacart, and Uber Eats tipping flows

### Definition & Ideation
- Mapped the end-to-end tipping journey across pre-order, checkout, and post-delivery
- Identified 4 key intervention points for improving tip rates
- Workshopped solutions with cross-functional stakeholders
- Prioritized using an impact-effort matrix

### Design & Iteration
- Designed A/B test variants for checkout tipping UI
- Prototyped post-delivery tipping flow with rating integration
- Ran 2 rounds of unmoderated usability testing (n=24)
- Refined copy and interaction patterns based on comprehension metrics

## Solution

The redesign touched three moments in the customer journey:

### Smart Defaults at Checkout
Replaced flat-dollar defaults with percentage-based suggestions anchored to order size. Added contextual messaging about how tips support drivers, increasing perceived value of tipping.

### Post-Delivery Prompt
Introduced a well-timed push notification and in-app prompt after delivery completion, letting customers adjust their tip based on actual experience. Included driver name and delivery details to personalize the moment.

### Transparency Layer
Added clear confirmation of tip distribution timing and a breakdown visible in order history. Customers could see exactly when their tip reached the driver, building trust in the system.

## Outcome

- Tip attachment rate increased from 52% to 68% (+31% relative lift)
- Average tip amount increased by 18% across tipped orders
- Post-delivery tip adjustments added incremental revenue for drivers
- Driver satisfaction NPS improved by 12 points in post-launch survey
- Feature rolled out to all Walmart delivery markets within 3 months
