After Liquidity, marketing messages could live inside ads creation. The next job was scale: get them onto more surfaces, for more kinds of businesses, without sending everyone through Ads Manager.

Three kinds of messages already run through WhatsApp via Meta. Trigger send is order status — confirmed, shipped, delivered. Audience send is the campaign: a list, a coupon, a Valentine’s Day or Black Friday sale. Service messages are what happens after the purchase, when someone has a question or a request about that item.

<WhatsAppPaths />

The strategy was to meet businesses where they already work.

## Meta Business Suite, not Ads Manager

Ads Manager is the full tool for larger advertisers. Meta Business Suite is built for small and medium businesses. Marketing messages are most common on WhatsApp, so 1P buying meant creating and sending them inside MBS.

Trigger send is operational. A business sets up those order statuses in MBS. When they sell on their site, Shopify, or another store, the event can fire a WhatsApp message so the customer gets the update there.

Audience send is the marketing version — a campaign to people they already have, not a status on an order.

I focused on audience send. I also helped a lot on trigger send: the first launch, and later ideation for a future where AI helps businesses create both kinds of send.

## Campaigns on Shopify and WooCommerce

3P buying is the same audience-send problem on two platforms we work with closely. Shopify already had APIs for trigger send — merchants could turn on WhatsApp order tracking from the store they already run. What we did not have was campaigns from that same place.

Adding audience send on Shopify and WooCommerce is not the most refined surface in the stack. It is still a large piece of business for Meta, which is why it belongs in this story.

## When help started costing money

Service messages used to be unmonetized. In late September or early October they were remonetized. Businesses needed to hear that before they hit a bill they did not expect.

That work sat with the billing team inside monetization. I spent a lot of time on the communication strategy: email, in-app, and other routes. These newer projects are AI-forward. I used AI agents with the team to work through the options — a way to get to a strategy we could ship, not the product itself.

The through-line for this assignment is not three separate products. It is one bet: marketing messages show up where the business already is, including the paths that used to be free.
