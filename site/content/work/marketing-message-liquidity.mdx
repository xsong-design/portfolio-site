## Overview

Marketing Message Liquidity addresses the challenge of helping businesses efficiently manage and optimize their messaging delivery across WhatsApp Business Platform. The project focused on creating a system that balances message throughput, quality, and cost — giving businesses visibility and control over how their marketing messages reach customers.

## Context & Problem

Businesses on the WhatsApp Business Platform were struggling with opaque delivery systems. They had limited insight into why messages were throttled, failed, or delivered slowly. This created frustration and eroded trust, especially for enterprises running time-sensitive campaigns.

Key challenges included:
- No visibility into delivery pipeline health
- Unclear feedback loops between message quality and delivery capacity
- Fragmented controls spread across multiple surfaces
- Businesses over-sending low-quality messages, degrading ecosystem health

## Process

The design process spanned discovery, definition, and iterative delivery across two quarters.

### Research & Discovery
- Conducted 12 interviews with enterprise messaging customers
- Analyzed support ticket patterns related to delivery issues
- Mapped the existing mental models businesses had about message delivery
- Benchmarked against email deliverability tools (SendGrid, Mailchimp)

### Definition & Ideation
- Synthesized research into key opportunity areas
- Ran collaborative design sprints with PM and engineering
- Explored multiple interaction models for surfacing delivery health
- Defined progressive disclosure strategy for complexity management

### Design & Iteration
- Created high-fidelity prototypes in Figma
- Tested with 8 enterprise customers across 3 rounds of usability testing
- Iterated on information hierarchy based on task-completion metrics
- Collaborated closely with engineering on technical feasibility

## Solution

The final design introduced three interconnected surfaces:

### Delivery Health Dashboard
A real-time overview showing message throughput, quality scores, and delivery capacity. Businesses can see at a glance whether their messaging is healthy and what actions could improve it.

### Quality Feedback Loop
An inline system that connects message quality metrics directly to delivery outcomes. When quality drops, businesses see the direct impact on their sending capacity with clear, actionable guidance.

### Capacity Controls
A simplified interface for managing sending limits, scheduling, and prioritization — replacing the previously fragmented controls with a unified experience.

## Outcome

- 34% reduction in delivery-related support tickets within the first quarter
- 22% improvement in message quality scores across participating businesses
- Enterprise customer satisfaction score increased from 3.2 to 4.1 (out of 5)
- System adopted as the default delivery management interface for all WhatsApp Business API customers
