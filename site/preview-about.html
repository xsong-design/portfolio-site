About.

Portrait photo

I'm Xiao — a product designer with an industrial design foundation from RISD, currently building digital experiences across the tech industry. Most recently at DoorDash designing their 0→1 Parcels platform, and before that at Meta working on enterprise messaging tools for WhatsApp and Ads Manager.

My work sits at the intersection of complex systems and human clarity. I've designed workflows for performance marketers at Meta, tipping experiences for delivery drivers at Walmart, and helped LiveControl achieve product-market fit and a $30M Series A. I'm drawn to hard problems — the ones where business incentives compete, user mental models don't match the product model, and the right answer isn't obvious.

I believe the best design work happens when you understand what every stakeholder needs, not just the user in front of you. Whether it's aligning three organizations at Meta or navigating the competing needs of customers, drivers, and the business at Walmart — the craft is in the clarity.

Design Philosophy
01
Complexity is the material, not the enemy
Enterprise products are inherently complex. I don't simplify by hiding — I simplify by creating clear structure. The goal is comprehension, not reduction.
02
Alignment before artifacts
The hardest design problems aren't about pixels. They're about getting three organizations to agree on what "good" looks like. I invest in framing before I invest in fidelity.
03
Transparency builds trust
When users understand how a system works, they trust it. I design for transparency — showing the "why" behind algorithmic decisions, budget allocations, and automated actions.
04
Craft at every layer
Interaction choreography, typographic hierarchy, motion design — craft isn't a phase, it's a standard. Every surface should feel intentional, from the information architecture to the hover state.
